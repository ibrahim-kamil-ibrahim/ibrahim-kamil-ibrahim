About Ibrahim Kamil Ibrahim

Ibrahim Kamil Ibrahim is a software developer based in Dire Dawa, Ethiopia, working across web, mobile, and desktop platforms to build practical, real-world applications. As Co-Founder at ONECO, he focuses on turning ideas into digital products that solve everyday problems for people and businesses.

His work spans the full development lifecycle — from planning and interface design to writing clean, maintainable code and shipping working software. He is continuously learning modern tools and technologies, with a particular interest in full-stack development, mobile applications, and building efficient software solutions from the ground up.

Ibrahim approaches every project with a problem-solving mindset: understanding what a person or business actually needs, then building something that works reliably and can grow over time.
